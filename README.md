## Technical Checklist for Evaluating Landscapers

### 1. Licence and Insurance
- Confirm the landscaper is licensed and insured
- The company describes itself as fully insured and licensed, and as registered builders and landscapers in NSW.

### 2. Design
- Confirm the design covers site, soil, aspect and budget
- Landscape design aims to understand each client's needs and uses sustainable and naturally appealing materials and plants, with plans covering hardscaping, pools, planting, lighting and more. Designers consider functionality, site orientation, local weather, budget and personal style.

### 3. Construction Scope
- Confirm what is built in-house
- Landscape construction covers timber decking, paving, retaining walls, garden edging, turf installation, artificial grass, planter boxes and custom outdoor sheds. Related work listed in quotes includes site preparation, excavation, fencing, soil analysis and amendments, irrigation design, grading and drainage, concreting and pergolas.

### 4. Process and Communication
- Confirm the consultation, quote, approvals and project contacts
- The process starts with an initial consultation of up to 90 minutes, where the team assesses the architecture, aspect, soil condition and existing elements, and takes photos and measurements. The design phase and quote follow, considering the existing landscape, budget and lifestyle, with multiple rounds of revisions and approvals. Construction begins once the design is finalised and the paperwork is complete, with a Project Manager and site Foreman as primary contacts and regular progress reports.

### 5. Approvals and Maintenance
- Confirm council requirements and aftercare
- Most designs do not need council approval, but a significant amount of new hardscape may be treated as major development needing City Council approval. Bringing photos, sketches or plans to the first meeting helps. Garden maintenance covers hedge trimming, mowing, weeding, spraying, mulching, fertilising and irrigation systems, for regular clients and one-off visits, and the company likes to maintain the gardens it builds so they fill out to their potential.
